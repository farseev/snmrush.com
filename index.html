What is SNMRush?

SNMRush (said "S-N-M rush") is an SEO, AEO and GEO agency. The name stands for Search, Neural answers and Mentions: we help brands rank in search results, get cited in AI answers from ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews, and earn brand mentions. SOMIN, an AI audience-research platform, is our technology partner for finding the real questions audiences ask.

The problem

Generative AI changed who gets seen, and most content was not built for it

Buyers now ask an assistant before they ask a search box, and the assistant replies with a short synthesis that names a handful of brands. If yours is not among them, you were never in the room. Rankings still matter, because many AI systems ground their answers in web results, but a page-one position no longer guarantees you are the source that gets quoted.

At the same time, AI-written content has flooded every category with the same safe paragraphs. When every brand publishes the same answer, engines have no reason to prefer yours. Visibility now depends on saying something specific, true and useful to a question people genuinely ask, and saying it in a form machines can lift cleanly.

About

Why we are called SNMRush

SNMRush, said "S-N-M rush", is named after the three places a brand now needs to be found: Search, Neural answers and Mentions. Search is the results page. Neural answers are the responses written by AI assistants and AI Overviews. Mentions are what others say about you, which both of the first two increasingly rely on.

We started SNMRush because clients kept asking the same question: why does the AI recommend my competitor? The honest answer was rarely technical. It was usually that the competitor had answered the buyer's real question clearly, somewhere credible, and we had not.

So we built an agency around listening first. We partner with SOMIN to hear the questions audiences voice, then do the patient work of search, structure and reputation that gets those answers found. We are part of a network of seven sister brands, all independent, all working with SOMIN.

FAQ

Questions, answered

What do SEO, AEO and GEO mean?

SEO, search engine optimisation, is about ranking in traditional results. AEO, answer engine optimisation, is about being the source an assistant or featured answer uses for a direct question. GEO, generative engine optimisation, is about being cited and described accurately in generated answers from tools like ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews. SNMRush treats them as one programme.

Is SNMRush connected to Semrush?

No. SNMRush is an independent agency with no affiliation to Semrush or any SEO software vendor. Our name stands for Search, Neural answers and Mentions, the three surfaces where we build visibility. We use a range of tools, and our technology partner is SOMIN, an AI audience-research platform.

Can you guarantee my brand will appear in ChatGPT or AI Overviews?

No one can honestly guarantee that. AI engines do not publish ranking rules, answers vary between sessions, and models change often. What we can do is improve the factors that are well supported: strong rankings, clear answer-first content, consistent entity information and credible third-party mentions, then measure your citation rate over time.

How do you measure AI visibility?

We build a fixed panel of prompts based on real audience questions, run them across ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews on a schedule, and record whether you are mentioned, cited or linked, and how you are described. We report that alongside search rankings and referral traffic from AI tools.

Why does SNMRush use SOMIN?

Because AI answers respond to questions, and the best source of real questions is real conversation. SOMIN shows the questions, tensions and language audiences use in public posts, with evidence attached. That lets us write content that answers what people actually ask, rather than what a keyword list guesses.

How long before we see results?

Technical fixes and restructured pages can be picked up within weeks once they are crawled. Rankings and AI citations usually take a few months to settle, and earned mentions build steadily. We set a baseline in the audit so every change is measured against where you started.
