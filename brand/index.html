Logo

Three stacked bars, one for each of Search, Neural answers and Mentions, with the top bar lit in signal yellow like the answer that gets chosen. Download: logo.svg · logo-dark.svg · mark.svg

  • Keep clear space equal to the height of the mark on every side.
  • Minimum size: 24 px tall on screen. Use the mark alone below 120 px wide.
  • Never recolour, stretch, outline or add effects. Use the dark version on #0F3D2E and photography.
  • Pair with the SOMIN partner lockup only as “Technology partner SOMIN”, never merged into the SNMRush logo.
