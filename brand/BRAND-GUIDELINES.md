# SNMRush brand guidelines (v1.0, October 2026)

**Positioning:** The visibility agency that starts from real audience questions, so brands get ranked, cited and mentioned in the answer era.
**Tagline:** Search, Neural answers, Mentions. One rush.
**Domain / contact:** snmrush.com · ask@snmrush.com
**Technology partner:** SOMIN (somin.ai). Show as "Technology partner SOMIN" with the two-tone SOMIN wordmark (SO teal #2DBF9C, MIN dark/white), never merged into our logo.

## Logo
Three stacked bars, one for each of Search, Neural answers and Mentions, with the top bar lit in signal yellow like the answer that gets chosen.
Files: `brand/logo.svg` (light backgrounds), `brand/logo-dark.svg` (dark backgrounds), `brand/mark.svg` (icon), `favicon.svg`.
Clear space = height of the mark. Minimum 24 px tall. No recolouring, stretching, outlines or effects.

## Typography
- Headlines: Outfit 700, tracking -0.03em, sentence case
- Body: Inter 400/500, 17 px / 1.65
- Labels: Inter 600, 12–13 px, uppercase, 0.2em tracking, accent colour
- Corner radius: 10px

## Personality
- precise
- candid
- curious
- energetic

## Voice
Do:
- Lead with the direct answer
- Say what is known and what is uncertain about AI engines
- Use worked examples and checklists

Don't:
- Promise guaranteed AI rankings
- Use hype words or vague growth claims
- Imply any link to SEO software vendors

## Imagery
Painterly landscapes of stacked cards, answer bubbles and beacons in signal yellow on deep forest green. No screenshots, no robots, no text in images. All imagery generated with SOMIN image generation; no text in images, no stock, no identifiable real people.

## Calls to action
Every primary CTA ("Get an AI visibility audit") opens an email to ask@snmrush.com.
